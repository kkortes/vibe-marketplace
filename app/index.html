Components for the web, without the ceremony.

Every component here is plain HTML living at a URL. Point a <component> tag at one and it renders — no install, no build step, nothing to keep in sync.

The #v3 pins that render to revision three, for good. Leave it off and the tag renders whatever the latest revision happens to be.
